## 3. GAME RULES & SCORING SYSTEM

### 3.1 General Game Rules
- Applicable standard Carom Billiards rules and specific supplementary regulations established by the SEHC Organizing Committee.
- Referees are officially assigned and coordinated by the Organizing Committee.

### 3.2 Match Format & Points
- **Match Format:** Each match is played in **Best of 3 sets (Bo3)**. The first team to win **2 sets** wins the match.
- **Scoring and Inning Limits by Round:**
  - **Preliminary Round to Semi-finals:** **20 points / 12 innings / set**.
  - **3rd Place Match and Finals:** **25 points / 15 innings / set**.

### 3.3 Player Selection Protocol for Each Set
- **Set 1:** The team selects any 02 players to compete.
- **Set 2:** The remaining 02 players of the team compete.
- **Set 3 (if necessary):** Each team selects only **01 player** to compete, wherein:
  - Team A selects its own player.
  - Team B nominates the opponent's player (and vice versa).

### 3.4 Alternate Inning Play Rule
- During doubles play, the two players on a team alternate turns at the table until an error/fault occurs, at which point the turn passes to the opposing team.
- *Example:* Player A plays and scores; upon completion of Player A's scoring inning, Player B takes the next inning for the team; then it returns to Player A. This rotation continues until a fault ends the team's inning.

### 3.5 Inning Expiration & Shootout Rules
- During a set, the team reaching the designated target score within the inning limit wins that set.
- If neither team reaches the target score after the maximum allowed innings (12 innings in preliminary to semi-finals, or 15 innings in finals/3rd place):
  - The team with the higher score is awarded the set win.
  - In case of a tie score upon inning expiration: a **break-shot shootout** will be conducted. Each side performs 01 break shot until a score difference is achieved; the team scoring higher wins the set. The team that played second during regular play breaks first in the shootout.

### 3.6 Attire and Equipment Regulations
- **Dress Code:**
  - Players must wear neat, polite sport attire: collared shirts, long trousers, and sports shoes. Uniform colors for team identification are highly encouraged. Shirts must be tucked in.
  - Short pants, ripped jeans, offensive graphics, and sandals/slippers are strictly prohibited.
  - Hard metal jewelry (sharp rings, necklaces, watches) must not be worn on table grounds; violators will receive a warning/yellow card and must step off to remove items.
- **Equipment:**
  - Players may equip and bring their own personal cues and compliant chalk/accessories.

## 4. DISCIPLINE, COMPLAINTS & PROCEDURES

### 4.1 Attendance and Default Rules
- All athletes must carry their Citizen ID or SEHC Employee ID Card for verification prior to matches.
- Players must report to their assigned table on time. The Organizing Committee will announce teams over PA and contact captains directly.
- Teams arriving more than **10 minutes** late without prior approved justification will forfeit the match (**Default 0 - 2 sets**, score recorded as 0/20 or 0/25).
- If athlete impersonation or ineligible personnel is detected, the violating team forfeits the match, has all prior achievements annulled, and is disqualified from the tournament.

### 4.2 Sportsmanship & Behavioral Discipline
- All players must strictly respect and follow the referee's instructions and whistles.
- Any impolite, provocative, or uncultured behavior will be subject to strict disciplinary sanctions.
- The Organizing Committee reserves the right to issue warnings, fines, suspensions, or permanent bans for any individual or team that damages the tournament's image or violates the Regulations.

### 4.3 Complaints Protocol
- Official complaints must have clear proof and be submitted in writing via the referee team immediately after the conclusion of the disputed set.
- Referees and court supervisors will compile a formal report for the Organizing Committee to review and decide upon immediately.
- Groundless or false complaints will be rejected and subject to disciplinary action against the appealing party.

### 4.4 Scoresheet Sign-off
- At the end of each match, team captains or designated representatives must report to the referee table to sign the official match scoresheet.
- Verify all scores and inning counts carefully before signing; once signed, all subsequent complaints regarding scores and outcomes become null and void.

### 4.5 General Terms
- All registered teams must strictly abide by these Regulations.
- The Organizing Committee reserves all rights to amend and supplement these Regulations as necessary to suit practical conditions.
