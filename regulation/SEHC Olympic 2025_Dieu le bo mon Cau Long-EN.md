## 3. RULES OF PLAY & SCORING SYSTEM

### 3.1 Rules of Play and Scoring
- The competition follows the current official Badminton Rules issued by the Vietnam Badminton Federation (VBF) and Badminton World Federation (BWF), combined with specific regulations established by the SEHC Organizing Committee.
- **Number of Sets and Point Target:**
  - **Each match is played in Best of 3 sets (Bo3): the first side to win 2 sets wins the match.**
  - **Each set is played to 15 points (applicable to all rounds, including the 3rd Place matches and Finals).**
  - **Deuce Rule:** In the event of a 14 - 14 tie, the pair that gains a 2-point lead first wins the set (e.g., 16 - 14, 17 - 15... max 21).
  - The final score of each match is recorded directly on the official match scoresheet.

### 3.2 Equipment and Attire
- **Official Shuttlecock:** Vinastar shuttlecock (blue tube/box) – supplied and provided on court by the Organizing Committee.
- **Player Attire:**
  - Athletes must wear appropriate sportswear suitable for high-intensity athletic movement.
  - Doubles partners are highly encouraged to wear matching uniform colors for easy identification and professional team appearance.
  - All athletes are strictly forbidden from wearing metal jewelry (necklaces, rings, bracelets, watches, etc.) onto the court; violators will receive a reminder/yellow card and must leave the court to remove the items.
- **Rackets & Personal Gear:**
  - Athletes are responsible for preparing their own rackets, court shoes, and personal supporting equipment.
  - The Organizing Committee is not responsible for broken rackets or damaged gear during the course of a match.
  - If equipment failure occurs and the pair cannot resolve the issue within 05 minutes to resume play, that pair will forfeit the match.

### 3.3 Umpires & Court Referees
- Umpires and court supervisors are assigned and appointed by the Organizing Committee.
- The decision of the assigned match umpire is final on the court.

## 4. DISCIPLINE, COMPLAINTS & GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 4.1 Roll Call & Walkover Rules
- All athletes must bring their Citizen ID card (CCCD) or SEHC Employee Badge for verification against the official registration roster prior to each match.
- Athletes must be present at their assigned court before the scheduled match time. The Organizing Committee will announce matches via PA speakers and communicate directly with team captains.
- If a pair fails to appear on the court **10 minutes** past the scheduled match time, they will be declared a walkover (forfeit loss with a score of 0 - 15).
- Any case of personnel fraud (substitute players, false employee credentials) will result in an immediate forfeit loss, revocation of all prior results, and disqualification from the entire tournament.

### 4.2 Sportsmanship and Conduct
- On court, all athletes must show absolute respect and follow the instructions and signals of the referee/umpire.
- Any unsportsmanlike behavior, vulgar language, or disrespect towards officials or opponents will be strictly disciplined.
- The Organizing Committee retains full authority to issue warnings, penalties, match disqualifications, or suspensions against any individual or team violating tournament ethics or regulations.

### 4.3 Grievance and Appeal Procedure
- Any official protest or complaint must be submitted immediately upon match conclusion through the umpire/supervising table with clear evidence.
- The court supervisor and umpire team will prepare a formal report for immediate review by the Organizing Committee.
- Baseless or malicious complaints will be dismissed and may result in disciplinary action against the appealing party.

### 4.4 Scoresheet Confirmation
- Immediately after each match finishes, team captains or player representatives must report to the secretariat table to sign the official match scoresheet.
- Supervisors and secretariats must accurately record the final scores and notes on the scoresheet.
- Once signed by team representatives, the scoresheet is final and binding; no further appeals or adjustments will be considered.

### 4.5 General Provisions
- All participating pairs and athletes must strictly adhere to the provisions outlined in these regulations.
- The Organizing Committee reserves the right to amend, adjust, or supplement these regulations whenever necessary to ensure the tournament's overall success and fairness.
